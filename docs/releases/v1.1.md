# v1.1 "Measured"

**Tagged** 4 Nov 2026 at `4c62484` ([GitHub Release](../../../../releases/tag/v1.1)). A checkpoint on the way to [v2.0 "XLR8"](v2.0.md): what has been **measured** since [v1.0](v1.0.md) (tagged at
`8b5d079`, 4 Nov 2026). The app, the images, the gate and the workflows haven't changed since that tag. The one
pipeline change is infrastructure: giraffe as a cloud align option ([#197](../../../../pull/197)). Otherwise v1.1 adds
numbers, the tools that produced them, and docs. Full detail is in [v2.0.md](v2.0.md), the cumulative XLR8 page.

## Measured

- **Cost × turnaround over the two sizing knobs** ([#221](../../../../pull/221)). 25 Fargate runs of HG003 30x
  chr20 over align vCPUs (1, 2, 4) × calling shards (1, 2, 4, 8):
  - **Cheapest:** 2 vCPU × 4 shards, 6.95¢ in 37 min.
  - **Fastest:** 4 vCPU × 4 shards, 7.78¢ in 24 min.
  - **1 vCPU** is both the slowest and the dearest.
  - **Queue waits:** median 21.5 s and p95 33.7 s over 216 jobs, plus one Fargate Spot placement wait of 548 s.
  - **Gate:** PASS on all 25 runs, in fail mode.
  - The figure is in [docs/figures](../figures/README.md), and how the grid ran is in its
    [design note](../figures/cost-surface-grid.md).
- **The right-sizing saving on a real sample** ([#222](../../../../pull/222), measuring
  [#174](../../../../pull/174)):
  - **Calling cost:** the variant-calling subtotal fell from $0.0157 to about $0.0114 a run (−27%), about 6% of
    a run, half the estimate.
  - **Why only half:** at 1 vCPU the bcftools shards took 1.6-1.8× as long.
- **A real-size GPU DeepVariant run** ([#223](../../../../pull/223), [#139](../../../../issues/139)):
  - **The run:** HG003 30x chr20 on a g6.xlarge (NVIDIA L4) took 46.5 min, with gate PASS.
  - **Where the time goes:** in the 1,200 s DeepVariant job, `make_examples` is 84%. The GPU was busy 4% of the job
    and 32% of `call_variants`.
  - **Cost:** about $0.55 a run, against about $0.072 with bcftools, so bcftools stays the default caller.
- **DeepVariant batch size × shards on the local GPU** ([#216](../../../../pull/216)):
  - **Shards set the time:** on HG003 chr20 on an RTX 3080, the stage takes about 8 min at 16 shards and 13.5-16
    min at 4.
  - **Batch size:** 256-512 is about 20% faster in `call_variants` than the default 1,024.
  - **Calls:** they differ by at most 2 PASS records between identical runs (GPU nondeterminism).
- **Release notes brought up to date** ([#222](../../../../pull/222)). The work applied on 3 Nov is now
  described with its checks:
  - per-run overrides ([#192](../../../../pull/192));
  - the quality gate in fail mode with a `smoke` profile ([#195](../../../../pull/195));
  - alignments expiring at 180 days ([#190](../../../../pull/190));
  - the run-record fix ([#204](../../../../pull/204)).

  Their code was already in the v1.0 tag.

## Graph alignment in the cloud

- **`aligner=giraffe` on Fargate** ([#197](../../../../pull/197), the align option of [#191](../../../../pull/191)): vg giraffe
  1.55.0 aligns the reads to the HPRC v1.1 chr20 graph, with haplotype sampling (kmc 3.2.4), in its own image.
  The rest of the run is unchanged.
  - **Smoke:** `CLOUD019-giraffe-20261104T102837Z` SUCCEEDED in 5 min 5 s, gate PASS (`smoke` profile), exactly the
    10 planted SNPs and 0 indels. The run record names aligner giraffe, the graph `hprc-v1.1-mc-grch38.chr20.gbz`
    with haplotype sampling, vg 1.55.0 and kmc 3.2.4.
  - **Real chr20:** `REAL-HG003-35x-chr20-giraffe-20261104T103431Z` SUCCEEDED in 53.5 min.
    - 98.74% of reads mapped; PASS SNPs 111,515, indels 20,357, Ti/Tv 1.86 (bcftools); gate PASS (`default`, fail
      mode).
    - The align job waited **14.6 min** for a Fargate Spot placement at 4 vCPU / 8 GiB: the second long wait at
      that size, after the cost grid's 548 s.
    - It then ran 1,463 s (CPU 3,436 s, peak 3.85 GB), against 814 s for bwa-mem2 on the same sample on 2 Nov.
  - **Chromosome-scale only:** haplotype sampling needs the whole sample, so the genome route (v1.3,
    [#229](../../../../pull/229)) refuses `aligner=giraffe`.
