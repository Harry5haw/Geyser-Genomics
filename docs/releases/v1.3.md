## The large route ([#225](../../../../pull/225) app, [#229](../../../../pull/229) infrastructure)

`Choose_Route` sends an upload of **5 GB or more** (`input_bytes`: the trigger adds up the mates' sizes) down the
large route. Smaller uploads keep today's path, with the same jobs.

```
Check_* → Set_Run_Settings → Choose_Route
  < 5 GB → QC_and_Align (as before)
  ≥ 5 GB → Check_Large_Aligner (bwa-mem2 only) →
           Parallel( QC_Large ‖ Preflight → Read_Manifest → Align_Chunks (Map) → Align_Merge )
→ Choose_Caller → region shards (Map) → merge → gate → outputs (unchanged)
```

| Step | What it does | Size |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `Preflight` | One streaming pass over the raw reads: gzip integrity, FASTQ shape, mates in step, read count. It deals the reads round-robin into N chunks (at most one read apart, mates kept together, `pigz -1`), and writes a manifest and a kept report, `qc_reports/<id>.preflight.json`. A bad input fails here, in minutes, before any alignment | 8 vCPU / 16 GiB / 200 GB disk |
| `Align_Chunks` | A Map over the manifest: `<aligner> mem | fixmate | sort` per chunk, **no duplicate marking** (a duplicate's twin can be in another chunk) | 8 vCPU / 32 GiB |
| `Align_Merge` | `samtools merge` of the chunk BAMs **read in place** (presigned HTTPS URLs, no local copy), then markdup once over every read, then CRAM + `.crai` and stats: **exactly the single-job align's outputs and run-record part** | 8 vCPU / 16 GiB / 200 GB disk |
| `QC_Large` | fastp on the whole input, alongside alignment | 8 vCPU / 16 GiB / 200 GB disk |

- **Region-streamed calling:**
  - Both routes record the CRAM's ETag on its `.crai`.
  - A calling shard reads only its region (htslib `URL##idx##crai`) when the ETags match, and downloads the CRAM as
    before when they don't, when the CRAM is old, or when streaming fails.
  - That turns ~180 downloads of a ~17 GB CRAM into region reads.
- **Only bwa-mem2 takes the large route.** giraffe stays chromosome-scale, because its haplotype sampling needs the
  whole sample's k-mer counts. Anything else fails with `AlignerNotOnLargeRoute` before any job.
- **No new compute:** one new app job definition with a 200 GiB disk; the existing environments with their 48 vCPU
  caps; no compute-environment, queue, bucket, VPC, NAT or IAM-boundary change. The applied plan was
  **1 to add, 4 to change, 0 to destroy**, exactly as written on the PR before planning.

### Per-run parallelism (validated, as #192's `overrides`)

| Key | Default | Bounds |
| :--- | ---: | :--- |
| `chunk_count` | 24 | 2-96 |
| `align_concurrency` | 5 | 1 … ⌊(Spot cap − QC's 8 vCPU) ÷ 8⌋ = 5 |
| `calling_concurrency` | 4 | 1 … ⌊Spot cap ÷ shard vCPU⌋ = 48 |
| `merge_threads` | 8 | 1 … 8 |

- **The bounds come from the Fargate Spot cap (48 vCPU)**, so a genome stays on Spot prices.
- **Peak:** QC 8 + 5 × 8 chunks = 48 vCPU. The plan's 6 concurrent chunks became 5, because QC runs alongside
  (6 × 8 + 8 = 56 > 48).
- A faster genome only needs the cap raised (a user apply).

### The chunked route is equivalent to one align job

- **Synthetic data (the Docker E2E, single- and paired-end): identical** VCF records and flagstat totals.
- **Real data (REAL-HG003-30x-chr20, 8,114,368 pairs, one job vs 4 chunks): equivalent, not identical.**
  - **The same 16,228,736 primary reads** (none lost or duplicated).
  - **A handful of alignment decisions differ:** 20 supplementary alignments, 5 primary-mapped, 191 duplicates
    (0.012%).
  - **Calls:** 136,245 vs 136,244 records; 99.8% share the same site and alleles, and genotypes agree at 99.96%
    of those.
- **Accuracy is the same** (hap.py, GIAB v4.2.1 HG003, chr20 confident regions):

| | Single job | Chunked | Δ |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| SNP F1 (precision / recall) | 0.990193 (0.985925 / 0.994499) | 0.990158 (0.985869 / 0.994485) | 0.000035 |
| Indel F1 (precision / recall) | 0.949949 (0.955572 / 0.944392) | 0.949993 (0.955661 / 0.944392) | 0.000044 |

  That passes the rule written down before the run: |ΔF1| ≤ 0.0005 for SNPs and ≤ 0.001 for indels, and
  precision and recall each ≤ 0.001.
- **Why it isn't byte-identical:** bwa-mem estimates the paired-end insert-size distribution **per input batch**, so
  any re-batching shifts a few alignments. That includes chunking, and it includes a different thread count: a
  4-vCPU align already differs from a 16-thread one.
- **The documented tolerance** (`docs/design/genome-scale-app.md`): the same primary reads; duplicates and records
  within 0.1%; F1 within the rule above.
- A fixed bwa `-K` would make runs byte-reproducible ([#232](../../../../issues/232), Future). It isn't changed in
  v1.3, because it would move every baseline.

### Storage and timeouts

- **Raw reads:** uploads of **5 GB or more** move to **S3 Glacier Instant Retrieval after 30 days**. That's the
  user's decision: re-runs still read them with no restore, and smaller uploads stay in Standard. Nothing under
  `raw_reads/` expires.
- **Scratch:** the chunks (`intermediate/chunks/`) and chunk BAMs (`intermediate/aligned/`) expire after 3 days.
- **Timeouts:** the run's `TimeoutSeconds` goes from 12 h to **24 h**; per job, pre-flight 2 h, a chunk 1 h, the
  merge 4 h, QC_Large 2 h; calling shards are unchanged.

## Results

### Canaries

- **Smoke test on the large route:** `CLOUD019-20261104T135153Z-large2` passed: 10/10 planted SNPs, $0.046.
- **The chr20 canary on the large route:** REAL-HG003-30x-chr20 was forced down the large route (`input_bytes`
  5e9, `chunk_count` 4) as `REAL-HG003-30x-chr20-p3-20261104T135947Z-large4`. It **SUCCEEDED in 22.3 min, $0.17**
  at on-demand list prices. Every data check against the single-job baseline (grid slot p3) passed:
  - the same primary reads;
  - duplicates, depth, SNPs, indels and records all within the tolerance;
  - the gate passed;
  - the `.crai`'s `cram-etag` was present, and every shard read its region in place.

  hap.py against the baseline: SNP F1 0.990186 → 0.990158, indel F1 0.949949 → 0.949993. Both changes are inside
  the limits set before the run (|ΔF1| ≤ 0.0005 for SNPs, ≤ 0.001 for indels). One Spot interruption hit the
  pre-flight, and the retry recovered it. The queue wait was 152 s.

### HG003 30x whole genome (Google Brain NovaSeq PCR-free, 2×151, CC0; bwa-mem2 + bcftools)

Run `HG003-WGS30x-20261104T152854Z-grch38c30` **SUCCEEDED in 2 h 59 min** (15:28:55 to 18:27:39 UTC, 4 Nov
2026), on reference `GRCh38_no_alt.fa`, with the Fargate Spot cap at 128 vCPUs, `chunk_count` 30 (15 aligned at
once) and calling 48 shards at once.

| Stage | Time per job | Cost (on-demand list prices) |
| :--- | ---: | ---: |
| QC_Large | 14 min | $0.73 for these three together |
| Preflight | 52 min at 22% CPU (I/O-bound: the slowest step) | (in the $0.73) |
| Align_Chunks (30 chunks, 15 at a time) | 15-22 min each; 9.18 job-hours in all | $5.10 |
| Align_Merge | 26 min | (in the $0.73) |
| Calling (244 region shards, 48 at a time) | 0.7-10.2 min each | $0.67 |
| **Total** | **2 h 59 min** | **$6.50** (plan: ~$6.25 on-demand) |

$5.83 of the $6.50 ran on Fargate Spot, so the real bill is lower than the list-price total. There were 20 Spot
interruptions, all retried by the job definition and all successful. The longest queue wait was 26.6 min.

- **The gate: PASS, 11/11 checks** on the `default` profile. 718,619,796 reads, read_count_difference 0, mapped
  99.57%, properly paired 97.53%, duplicates 9.90%, mean depth 29.29x, breadth at 10x 93.0%, Ti/Tv 1.93. Breadth
  is measured over the whole reference, Ns included (5.32% of GRCh38 no-alt), so it can't go above 94.68%.
  Whether to exclude Ns from it, or use a genome profile, is a next item.
- **The calls:** 4,181,813 SNPs and 863,826 indels; the CRAM is 16.0 GB. **SLOT: hap.py against GIAB v4.2.1
  HG003 GRCh38 (chr1-22), run offline: SNP and indel F1.**
- **The checks:** read_count_difference 0 (the CRAM holds every input read, so none were lost in the chunks or
  the merge). All 244 shards streamed their region from the CRAM in place (0 fallbacks to a download).
- **Earlier starts:** two earlier starts were stopped on purpose. The first used the default reference
  (`chr20.fa`), the second calling concurrency 4. The reference itself was built beforehand by
  `scripts/build_reference_index.py` ([#239](../../../../pull/239)): 18.8 min, at most $0.51.
